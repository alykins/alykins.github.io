Dear Yasar, my sunshine,

I stitched the sun onto the front of this hoop for a very specific reason. To me, that is exactly who you are.

No matter how cold, chaotic, or stressful the rest of the world gets, the second I am around you, everything just settles down. You have this incredible, natural ability to bring light and massive energy into my life just by being yourself.

I am incredibly proud of the man you are, how hard you work, and how you look after the people around you. Every single thread in this piece was pulled while thinking about how incredibly lucky I am to have you as my teammate.

Keep this somewhere close to remind you of the impact you have on me every single day.

Yours forever and always, Alyshah.
